Gerbert of Aurillac · Epistolae

The Letters of Gerbert

❧ ❧ ❧

The letters of a schoolmaster who became pope: teacher at Reims, tutor to the emperor Otto III, and from 999 Pope Sylvester II. They ask for books, explain the abacus and the sphere, and follow the politics of the last Carolingians and the rising Capetians. This is Harriet Pratt Lattin's translation of 1961, with her introduction and notes and his papal privileges, made into a new ebook.

De hac editioneAbout this edition

Harriet Pratt Lattin, The Letters of Gerbert, with His Papal Privileges as Sylvester II (New York: Columbia University Press, 1961; Records of Civilization 60): the preface, introduction, all 264 letters and privileges with their notes, the four appendices, the comparative list of letter numbers, the bibliography and the index.

The ebook is made from the Princeton Theological Seminary Library's copy, scanned for the Internet Archive. The text was proofread against the page images, with italics, accents and note numbers restored; note numbers link to the notes and back, the printed page numbers are kept as page markers, and every page reference in the index is a link. Lattin's wording is unchanged. This edition was prepared with AI assistance (Claude, by Anthropic).

Lattin's translation was published in the United States in 1961 and its copyright was not renewed, so it is in the public domain in the United States. It may still be in copyright in other countries; check before downloading outside the US.
